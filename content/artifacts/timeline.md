# Timeline

`<Timeline>` shows events in order so the reader can follow how the work unfolded over time. Use it when the sequence of decisions or milestones matters as much as the final system.

- Component: `app/components/mdx/Timeline.tsx`
- Props:
- `<Timeline>` takes `children`
- `<Event>` takes `date`, `label`, and optional `note`
- When to use: for project phases, decision logs, migrations, and week-by-week progress
