# Callout

`<Callout>` highlights one short point that deserves extra attention without interrupting the flow of the article. Use it after a paragraph when you want the reader to notice an insight, risk, trade-off, or outcome.

- Component: `app/components/mdx/Callout.tsx`
- Props:
- `type`: `insight | warning | tradeoff | result`, defaults to `insight`
- `children`: the callout content
- When to use: for short emphasis blocks, not full sections or long explanations
