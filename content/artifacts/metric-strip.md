# Metric Strip

`<MetricStrip>` groups a few proof points so the reader can scan the result quickly. Use it when real numbers explain scale or outcome better than another paragraph.

- Component: `app/components/mdx/MetricStrip.tsx`
- Props:
- `<MetricStrip>` takes `children`
- `<Metric>` takes `label` and `value`
- When to use: near the top of a case study or in the results section with 2 to 4 real metrics
