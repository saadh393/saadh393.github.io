# Clip Encoders

`<ClipEncoders>` is an animated isometric illustration of CLIP's two encoders writing into one shared vector space. The reader picks an image and sees its vector land next to the matching caption.

- Component: `app/components/mdx/ClipEncoders.tsx`
- Geometry: `lib/isometric.ts`
- Props: none
- When to use: when introducing how CLIP compares images and text
